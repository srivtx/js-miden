# Research & References

## Academic Papers

1. **"Role-Based Access Control"** - Ferraiolo & Kuhn (1992)
   - Foundational RBAC paper from NIST
   - https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/role-based-access-control

2. **"The Protection of Information in Computer Systems"** - Saltzer & Schroeder (1975)
   - Classic paper on security principles including least privilege

## Industry Standards

1. **NIST SP 800-53**
   - Security controls for federal information systems
   - AC-3: Access Enforcement
   - AC-6: Least Privilege

2. **OWASP API Security Top 10**
   - API3:2019 - Excessive Data Exposure
   - https://owasp.org/www-project-api-security/

3. **GDPR Article 9**
   - Processing of special categories of personal data
   - Salary may be considered sensitive depending on jurisdiction

## Frameworks & Libraries

1. **casl** (npm)
   - Isomorphic authorization library
   - https://casl.js.org/

2. **accesscontrol** (npm)
   - Role and attribute based access control
   - https://github.com/onury/accesscontrol

3. **class-transformer** (npm)
   - Decorator-based transformation/serialization
   - Useful for DTO mapping
   - https://github.com/typestack/class-transformer

## Related Systems

- **Workday**: Enterprise HR system with complex RBAC
- **BambooHR**: SMB-focused with simpler permissions
- **Gusto**: Payroll/HR with strong compliance focus

## Articles

1. **"The Anatomy of a Data Breach"** - Verizon DBIR
   - Annual report on data breach causes
   - Privilege misuse is consistently in top 5

2. **"Salary Transparency"**
   - Some companies (Buffer, Whole Foods) publish all salaries
   - Different from accidental exposure - intentional transparency is a policy choice
