# Research & References

## Academic Papers

1. **"Detecting Near-Duplicates for Web Crawling"** - Manku, Jain & Sarma (2007)
   - Google Web crawler's approach to near-duplicate detection
   - https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//pubs/archive/33026.pdf

2. **"A Survey of Text Similarity Approaches"** - Gomaa & Fahmy (2013)
   - Comprehensive comparison of similarity algorithms

2. **"Building a Fraud Detection System"** - Netflix Tech Blog

2. **string-similarity** (npm)
   - Simple Jaccard/levenshtein implementations
   - https://github.com/aceakash/string-similarity

3. **@xenova/transformers**
   - Run transformer models in Node.js for embeddings
   - https://github.com/xenova/transformers.js

## Insurance Industry

1. **CLUE Database (Comprehensive Loss Underwriting Exchange)**
   - LexisNexis database tracking insurance claims history
   - Used to detect cross-company fraud

2. **ISO ClaimSearch**
   - Industry-wide fraud detection database
   - 1+ billion claims from 90%+ of US insurers

3. **NAIC Fraud Prevention**
   - National Association of Insurance Commissioners guidelines
   - https://content.naic.org/center_for_insurance_policy_and_research_fraud_prevention.htm
