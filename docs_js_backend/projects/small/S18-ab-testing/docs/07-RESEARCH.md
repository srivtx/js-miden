# 07-RESEARCH.md

## Citations and References

### Foundational Statistics

1. **Fisher, R. A. (1935). "The Design of Experiments"**
   - Oliver & Boyd, Edinburgh
   - The original text on randomized controlled experiments, analysis of variance, and significance testing.

2. **Kohavi, R., & Longbotham, R. (2017). "Online Controlled Experiments and A/B Testing"**
   - Encyclopedia of Machine Learning and Data Mining
   - Comprehensive survey of A/B testing in internet-scale companies.

3. **Kohavi, R., Deng, A., Frasca, B., Walker, T., Xu, Y., & Pohlmann, N. (2013). "Online Controlled Experiments at Large Scale"**
   - Proceedings of the 19th ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining
   - Describes Microsoft's experimentation platform (ExP) serving billions of users.

### Assignment and Randomization

4. **Tang, D., Agarwal, A., O'Brien, D., & Meyer, M. (2010). "Overlapping Experiment Infrastructure: More, Better, Faster Experimentation"**
   - Proceedings of the 16th ACM SIGKDD
   - Google's approach to layering experiments and hash-based assignment.

5. **Bakshy, E., Eckles, D., & Bernstein, M. S. (2014). "Designing and Deploying Online Field Experiments"**
   - Proceedings of the 23rd International Conference on World Wide Web
   - Discusses randomization units, spillover effects, and experimental design.

### Statistical Methods

6. **Deng, A., Lu, J., & Chen, S. (2016). "Continuous Monitoring of A/B Tests Without Pain"**
   - Proceedings of the 29th International Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems
   - Always-valid p-values and sequential testing for safe peeking.

7. **Deng, A., Xu, Y., Kohavi, R., & Walker, T. (2013). "Improving the Sensitivity of Online Controlled Experiments by Utilizing Pre-Experiment Data"**
   - Proceedings of the 6th ACM International Conference on Web Search and Data Mining
   - CUPED variance reduction technique.

8. **Johari, R., Pekelis, L., & Walsh, D. J. (2017). "Always Valid Inference: Bringing Sequential Analysis to A/B Testing"**
   - arXiv:1512.04922
   - Mathematical foundation for always-valid p-values.

### Industry Practice

9. **Xu, Y., Chen, N., Fernandez, A., Sinha, O., & Schuster, M. (2015). "From Infrastructure to Culture: A/B Testing Challenges in Large Scale Social Networks"**
   - Proceedings of the 21st ACM SIGKDD
   - Facebook's experimentation challenges at scale.

10. **Netflix Technology Blog: "A/B Testing at Netflix"**
    - https://netflixtechblog.com/a-b-testing-at-netflix-47d2fbc8fa01
    - Case studies on title artwork, recommendation algorithms, and UI tests.

### Ethics

11. **Salganik, M. J., et al. (2006). "Experimental Study of Inequality and Unpredictability in an Artificial Cultural Market"**
    - Science, 311(5762), 854-856
    - Foundational work on the ethics and unexpected consequences of experiments in social systems.
